02 / Multimodal article preview

Sample article / Fictional demonstration

It's okay not to know everything on day one.

On my first morning at university, I walked into the wrong building. Twice. I thought everyone else already knew where to go and who to talk to.

By Friday, I had learned something useful: asking a question is often the fastest way to feel less lost. You do not have to figure everything out alone.

Try this: Ask one classmate how their first week is going. You might discover you have more in common than you think.

04 / The idea

Made with students, not just for them.

“First Year, Figured Out” is a proposed five-week extracurricular project in which volunteer first-year students create English-language content for peers. The publication is both a real-world communication outcome and a setting for exploring students' experiences of choice, competence, and collaboration.
